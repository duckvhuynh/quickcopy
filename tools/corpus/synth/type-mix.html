The quiet return of the printed map

Phones know where we are. So why are paper maps selling again, and who is buying them?
By Helen Okafor · 12 min read

Sales of folded maps rose eighteen percent last year, according to the Cartographic Trade Survey. Most buyers were under thirty-five.

“A map shows you everything at once,” says one shop owner. A screen shows you only the next turn.

BEST SELLERS: COASTAL WALKS, NIGHT SKY, CITY CYCLE ROUTES
“People want to get lost on purpose.”

Publishers have noticed. Three new map series launch this spring, printed on waterproof stone paper.
